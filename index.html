Why trust these numbers?

Every calculator on XoftCal shows its formula and a fully worked example, uses standard US material sizes and densities (for example 145 lb per cubic foot for hot-mix asphalt, 10 sq ft per standard sod roll), and includes a sensible waste allowance where cutting is involved. Results are planning estimates — always confirm final quantities with your supplier — but they are the same math contractors and lumber yards use at the counter.

Built for real projects

Whether you are pricing a fence before a contractor quote, checking how many tons of asphalt a driveway really takes, or figuring curtain fullness so drapes look tailored instead of skimpy, start here: measure, calculate, and order once — not twice. New in-depth guides are published on the guides blog every day, each one linked to the calculator it supports.
