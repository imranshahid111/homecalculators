About CalcHomeLab

CalcHomeLab is a small, independent project with one job: answer "how much do I need?" accurately, fast, and for free.

Every calculator here was built from the same formulas used by lumber yards, sod farms, paving plants and pool suppliers in the USA, then checked against worked examples by hand. Each tool page shows its formula, an example with real numbers, measuring steps and the common mistakes that cause under-ordering — because we would rather explain the math than hide it.

The site is supported by advertising, which is why the calculators stay free with no sign-up and no paywall. We do not sell user data; the calculators run entirely in your browser.

Found a number you think is wrong? Tell us — corrections are how this site gets better.
